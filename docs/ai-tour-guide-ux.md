# AI Tour Guide UX Direction

## Product Vision

AI Tour Guide is a premium camera-first travel assistant.

The core promise:

> Point your camera at the world and understand what you are seeing.

The app should feel like a personal AI guide that recognizes places, explains why they matter, tells short stories, and suggests what to explore next.

---

## Language

Use English only for the current product version.

Do not add Georgian UI copy at this stage.

---

## Core UX Flow

```text
First launch
→ Onboarding
→ Get Started
→ Permission explanation
→ Guide tab
→ Tap to identify
→ Analyzing state
→ Recognition result
→ Short version / Tell me / More details
→ Map, History, or Profile
```

## First Launch UX

The first screen must be onboarding.

Do not show bottom tabs on onboarding.

Required content:

App name:
AI Tour Guide

Headline:
Discover the world with your camera

Subtitle:
Point your camera at a place, building, or object and let AI bring it to life with stories, history, and local insights.

Primary CTA:
Get Started

After tapping `Get Started`, continue to the permission explanation screen.
